# Code of Conduct

Nova is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and make space for people
with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
ban participants whose behavior harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer. Until a dedicated project contact is
published, use GitHub's private security advisory or account-reporting mechanisms where
appropriate. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
